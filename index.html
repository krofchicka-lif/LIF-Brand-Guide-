Voice — Tone Guardrails
What we don't say

Restraint is the
voice.

01 — Don't over-explain.

Elite decks trust the audience. State the point once and move on.

02 — Don't lead with philosophy.

Lead with the broken system. Earn the vision.

03 — "No incentives" — say it once.

Say it once and it's a feature. Say it three times and it sounds defensive.

04 — No corporate language.

Not "facilitate." Not "leverage." Not "drive engagement." Human words only.

05 — Don't mention competition.

LIF is its own category. There is nothing to compare to. Speak from that position.

+ Spelling

British/Canadian spelling throughout (behaviour, not behavior).

Voice & Tone
Writing Principles

Seven rules.
That's it.

  • Short sentences over long ones.
  • Active over passive.
  • Concrete over abstract.
  • Human over corporate.
  • One idea per sentence.
  • Earn the emotion — don't assert it.
  • British/Canadian spelling throughout.
Design Principle

"Humanity
without performance."

The brand should never announce emotion, explain emotion, over-frame emotion, or perform sincerity. It quietly witnesses human behaviour.

Don't

"Human connection changes everything."

Do

"This meant more than you know."

Don't

"A revolutionary new growth channel."

Do

"4 people came because Amy chose Rachel."

The second trusts the audience. The first performs for them.

Visual Identity — Typography
Typography

A pairing built for
editorial calm.

Primary · Headlines
Romana

A serif typeface with a distinctive cut in the curve of the lowercase j and f — harkening to cyclical, free-flowing waves. Used in regular (roman) weight only. Never bold. Set uppercase or sentence case for headings.

Visual Identity — Visual Art
Visual art

Cut, collaged,
handmade.

Graphic material should appear handmade — we love interesting shapes and solid colours. Use shapes that appear to be cut out and collaged. We love abstract, organic, natural shapes — especially circles and repetition. These compositions tie back to our logo and the cyclical spread of kindness.

Palettes should be simple and straightforward — 1–3 strong shades, built around the primary brand colours.

Reference — handmade compositions, primary palette, simple geometry

Strategy
Why no one built this

Four reasons
the gap existed.

01 — Incumbent incentive
The gift card industry was built on breakage. Incumbents had no incentive to look at post-redemption behaviour.
02 — Wrong direction
Every word-of-mouth play went the wrong direction. Referral programs replaced gratitude with coupons.
03 — Pattern unlegible
The Venmo pattern required Venmo to exist first. The playbook wasn't legible until it was proven.
04 — Urgent, but invisible
iOS14 made the problem urgent — and nobody had the infrastructure.

The future

As AI saturates the internet,
